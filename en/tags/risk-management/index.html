  • Setting the Trading System's Goal - Absolute Return ~10%/yr, Scrapping the Always-Fully-Invested Rule
    Yesterday I wrote that "stay positive even when the index drops" and "never leave cash idle" couldn't both be true, and that choosing between them was my call. As a first step, an intraday filter that briefly defers trades on sharp spikes and drops went live on the real account.
